Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JobMatch ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use JobMatch:

  • Account information: email address used to create your account
  • Resume data: text content of resumes you upload
  • Job application data: job descriptions, URLs, notes, and application statuses you add
  • Profile information: name, job title, career preferences you provide

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the JobMatch service
  • Generate AI-powered job match scores and analysis
  • Generate cover letters based on your resume and job descriptions
  • Provide career insights based on your job search activity

Third-Party Services

JobMatch uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: secure database and authentication. Data is stored encrypted at rest.
  • OpenAI API: used to analyze job descriptions and generate match scores and cover letters. Job descriptions and resume text are sent to OpenAI for processing.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security. Only you can access your own data. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

Children's Privacy

JobMatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
